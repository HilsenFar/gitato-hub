RL Live Tracker

A free coaching tracker for Rocket League on PC. It reads the game's official Stats API on your machine while you play, measures your habits against your own baseline, and coaches you one focus at a time.

Download for Windows Free · latest version · direct .zip

Windows SmartScreen will warn you, because this is an unsigned indie build. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". The LAES-MIG.txt in the zip walks you through everything else.

Measured, not generated

Around 30 behavioral metrics per match: kickoff first touches, touch power, boost discipline, movement. All computed from the telemetry the game itself broadcasts.

Your baseline is your own play. Never pro benchmarks.

Advice with sources

The written advice comes from a bank of quotes from published Rocket League coaching guides. Over 30 sources, cited in the app, so you can see whose advice you are getting and look it up yourself.

Works with EAC on

One setting in TAStatsAPI.ini, and Rocket League broadcasts match JSON on a local port on your PC. Nothing touches the game's process or memory.

Psyonix documents this channel for exactly this purpose.

Your data stays yours

  • No login, no account, nothing to sign up for.
  • It runs on your PC, and your telemetry stays there.
  • On first launch it asks once: "Join the test round?" Yes shares your match reports with the developer, to calibrate the coach. No means nothing is ever uploaded.
  • Every file it writes is plain JSON you can open and read yourself.
  • The rest of the network traffic: public rank lookups and a daily version check. That is the whole list.

Get started

  1. Download the zip and unpack it anywhere.
  2. Run the exe inside. Answer the test-round question however you like.
  3. Play ranked. Your first matches build your baseline, then the coaching starts.
RL Live Tracker is in an open test phase. Found a bug, or advice that feels off? Use the FEEDBACK button in the app. Requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and Rocket League on PC. A GITATO project.
